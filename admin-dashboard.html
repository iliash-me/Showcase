FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is happy to help.

Which frameworks are included?

Every purchase includes HTML (Bootstrap 5), React, Next.js, Vue 3, Angular and Laravel versions, plus the Figma design file.

Does it include a backend or API?

Vantage is a front-end template. The Laravel version includes authentication scaffolding and example controllers so you can connect your own data quickly.

Can I use it for a SaaS product?

Yes. If your end users pay to use the product (for example a subscription SaaS), you'll need an Extended License. Internal tools and client projects are covered by a Regular License.

Is the Figma file included?

Yes — the full Figma source with components, variants and color variables is included with every license.

Are updates free?

Absolutely. All future updates, including new dashboards, apps and framework upgrades, are free for life.

How do I get support?

Each purchase includes 6 months of premium support via our help desk. Most tickets are answered within one business day.
