FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is happy to help.

Does it use the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Replyo uses the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API from Meta — no QR-code sessions or unofficial libraries, which can get numbers banned.

Do I have to pay Meta for messages?

Meta charges messaging fees according to its WhatsApp Business pricing, billed directly to your Meta business account. Replyo doesn't add any per-message fees.

Can I send messages to anyone?

You can message contacts who have opted in. Business-initiated messages must use templates approved by WhatsApp, and you must follow WhatsApp's Business and Commerce policies.

Can I run it as a SaaS?

Yes — enable SaaS mode to sell plans to other businesses. This requires the Extended License.

Will it run on shared hosting?

It can, if your host supports SSL, cron jobs and long-running queue workers. A small VPS is recommended for large campaigns.

Do you help with setup?

Yes. The docs include a full Meta app setup guide, and installation is free with the Extended License.
