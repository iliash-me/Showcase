FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is happy to help.

What do I get when I purchase Nexora?

You get all 12 homepages, 80+ inner pages, 250+ UI blocks, the full SCSS source, a Gulp workflow, PHP contact form and detailed documentation.

Do I need coding knowledge to use it?

Basic HTML and CSS knowledge is enough to edit content. The documentation walks you through every step, from setup to deployment.

Can I use Nexora for client projects?

Yes. A Regular License covers one end product for you or one client. If your end users are charged, you'll need an Extended License.

Are future updates free?

Absolutely. Once you purchase Nexora, all future updates — including new demos and pages — are free forever.

Is it compatible with all browsers?

Nexora is tested on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge and Opera, on both desktop and mobile.

How do I get support?

Every purchase includes 6 months of premium support through our support portal. Most tickets are answered within 24 hours.
